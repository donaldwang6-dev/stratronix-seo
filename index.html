Core Business: Three Pillars

① On-Premise Private AI Hardware Appliances

All customer critical data stays on-device. No cloud transmission. No data leakage. Purpose-built for regulated industries where HIPAA, GDPR, and EU AI Act 2026 compliance is non-negotiable.

② Open-Source Tool Chain: OPENCLAW + UBUNTU + Open-Source AI Tools

We use open-source AI tools including OPENCLAW (the agent framework powering the STA-100, MIT license, MCP compatible) and UBUNTU. This drastically reduces the total cost of AI ownership — no vendor lock-in, no per-seat licensing fees, no subscription.

③ Highly Stable AI Low-Level Drivers

Proprietary AI inference optimization layer delivering 7×24 industrial-grade stability with 99.99% uptime SLA. Built for deployment scenarios where failure is not an option: hospitals, law firms, financial institutions, government agencies.
