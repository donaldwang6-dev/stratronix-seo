Why LATAM? Why Now?

Three forces are converging to make Latin America the next major market for private AI hardware:

  1. Data sovereignty laws are tightening. Brazil's LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados), Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law, Chile's Ley 19.628, and Uruguay's Ley 18.331 all impose strict rules on cross-border AI inference. Cloud-based AI services often cannot legally process sensitive data in these jurisdictions.
  2. SaaS AI costs are unsustainable. LATAM companies pay $200-$500 USD per month for cloud AI subscriptions — a significant share of an SME's operating budget. The STRATRONIX STA-100 PAA at $399 USD one-time pays for itself in 2-4 months.
  3. Connectivity gaps persist. Many LATAM businesses operate in regions with intermittent or expensive internet. The PAA's 100% offline capability means inference never stops, regardless of connectivity.

Country-Specific Compliance

🇧🇷 Brazil — LGPD

Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018) requires explicit consent for AI processing of personal data. The STA-100 PAA's local-only inference eliminates the consent issue entirely — data never leaves Brazil. We have opened Portuguese-language pages at /latam-br/china-ai.html.

🇲🇽 Mexico — LFPDPPP

Mexico's Ley Federal de Protección de Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares requires data minimization. Our local inference model satisfies this by design. Spanish-language resources: /latam-mx/china-ai.html.

🇨🇱 Chile — Ley 19.628

Chile's data protection law (currently being modernized to align with GDPR) requires consent for sensitive data processing. STRATRONIX's no-cloud architecture removes this concern. Chile resources: /latam-cl/china-ai.html.

🇦🇷 Argentina — Ley 25.326

Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Ley 25.326) requires data localization for sensitive information. The PAA's local-only mode is purpose-built for this requirement. Argentina resources: /latam-ar/china-ai.html.

🇨🇴 Colombia — Ley 1581

Colombia's Ley 1581 de 2012 (Habeas Data) is one of the strictest in LATAM. STRATRONIX's hardware isolation model is the most defensible architecture for Colombian enterprises. Resources: /latam-co/china-ai.html.

🇺🇾 Uruguay — Ley 18.331

Uruguay's Ley 18.331 has been a model for LATAM data protection since 2008. The PAA's offline-first design aligns with Uruguay's emphasis on data subject control. Resources: /latam-uy/china-ai.html.
