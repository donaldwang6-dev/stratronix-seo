PAA — Private AI-Agent Appliance Explained

The Private AI-Agent Appliance (PAA) is a new category of AI hardware pioneered by STRATRONIX. Unlike SaaS AI agents that send data to the cloud, PAA runs entirely on-premise in your office or data center. Use cases include:

  • 🏛️ Sovereign AI for governments (EU AI Act 2026 compliant)
  • 🏥 Private AI for healthcare (HIPAA/GDPR compliant)
  • 🏦 Private AI for finance (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • 🏭 Edge AI for manufacturing (factory floor, no cloud)
  • 📚 Private AI for education (student data stays on campus)
  • ⚖️ Legal AI for law firms (privileged data never leaves)
