What Is an Edge AI Device?

An edge AI device is a purpose-built hardware appliance that runs AI inference locally — at the network edge or within an organization's premises — without requiring cloud connectivity.

Unlike traditional AI deployments that rely on remote GPU servers, edge AI devices offer:

  • Low latency: Sub-100ms response times for real-time applications
  • Data privacy: Sensitive data never leaves the device
  • Offline operation: Functions without internet connectivity
  • Predictable costs: No per-query cloud LLM fees
  • Regulatory compliance: GDPR, HIPAA, FedRAMP, EU AI Act native support
