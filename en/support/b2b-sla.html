4. Hardware Replacement SLA

For hardware failures under warranty (FT-5x fault codes), STRATRONIX commits to:

  • Issue prepaid shipping label: within 1 business day of fault confirmation
  • Ship replacement unit: within 3 business days of receiving the faulty unit
  • Transfer configuration: configuration, models, and conversation history migrated to replacement unit automatically
  • Return shipping: original unit return shipping covered by STRATRONIX

For orders of 50+ units: STRATRONIX provides 1 spare unit per 50 units ordered, kept at Customer's site for immediate swap. Replacement unit shipping SLA reduced to 24 hours.

7. Customer Responsibilities

To be eligible for SLA coverage, Customer agrees to:

  1. Maintain SSH access enabled on all PAA units (or designate an alternate remote access method approved by STRATRONIX)
  2. Apply firmware updates within 30 days of release
  3. Provide accurate fault descriptions and diagnostic bundle outputs when reporting issues
  4. Maintain environmental conditions within operating specifications (temperature 0-35°C, humidity 10-90% non-condensing, clean power)
  5. Designate a primary technical contact and an escalation contact
  6. Not modify the OpenClaw OS firmware or hardware without STRATRONIX written approval

8. Exclusions

The following are excluded from SLA coverage:

  • Failures caused by Customer modification of hardware or firmware
  • Failures caused by operation outside environmental specifications
  • Failures caused by force majeure (natural disasters, war, government action)
  • Failures caused by third-party software installed by Customer
  • Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
  • Consumables (cables, external accessories)

9. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of:

  • Fault diagnostic data and root cause analyses
  • Customer's deployment architecture and use cases
  • STRATRONIX firmware source code (where disclosed for troubleshooting)
  • Any commercial terms not publicly available

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this SLA for [3 / 5] years.
