1. 2-Year Free Warranty

Every PAA comes with a 2-year warranty from the date of delivery. If your device fails due to manufacturing defects during this period, we will:

  1. Diagnose remotely within 24 hours of your support ticket
  2. Ship a replacement free of charge if remote fix doesn't work
  3. Pay return shipping for the defective unit via FedEx
  4. Restore your data from your backup snapshot (if you used the built-in backup feature)

What's covered: Hardware defects, firmware bugs, factory assembly issues.

What's not covered: Physical damage from drops, water damage, unauthorized modifications, third-party software conflicts. (Standard exclusions across the industry.)
