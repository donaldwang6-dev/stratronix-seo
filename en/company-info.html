📖 Company Story

Stratronix Technology was founded in Shenzhen in April 2026, pioneering the Private AI-Agent Appliance (PAA) category.

In the cloud-API-dominated AI era, enterprises face two major challenges: data sovereignty (sensitive data cannot go to the cloud) and deployment complexity (private deployment is expensive). STRATRONIX solves both with one piece of hardware: run LLM inference locally, data never leaves the device, 15-minute setup.

Built on the Apache 2.0 open-source OpenClaw framework, supporting Qwen / Llama / Mistral open-source LLMs. All source code, customer data, and runtime models stay on the customer's own hardware—no cloud dependency.
