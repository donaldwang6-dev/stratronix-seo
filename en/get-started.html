The 5 Steps

1Unbox and plug in (30 seconds)

Open the box. Take out the device (about the size of a Mac mini), the power adapter, and the quick-start card. Plug the power adapter into the wall.

2Connect to a screen (30 seconds)

Use any HDMI cable you have at home to connect the PAA to a TV or computer monitor. The PAA outputs 4K at 60Hz. Don't have HDMI? Use a USB-C to HDMI adapter (any smartphone charger cable works).

3Press the power button (10 seconds)

The power button is on the front of the device, top-right corner. Press once. The blue LED will light up. Wait about 30 seconds for the system to boot.

4Open the AI chat in your browser (2 minutes)

The desktop shows a Firefox icon. Double-click it. The browser automatically opens localhost:8080 — the AI chat interface. You can also use any device on the same WiFi network: from your phone or laptop, visit http://paa.local.

5Pick your first task (1 minute)

The home screen shows 8 ready-made task templates:

  • 💬 Chat with AI — Free-form conversation in your language
  • 📄 Summarize Document — Drag-and-drop any PDF, get a 1-page summary
  • 🌍 Translate — Translate text between 50+ languages locally
  • 💻 Write Code — Generate code in Python, JavaScript, SQL, and 30+ languages
  • 📧 Write Email — Professional emails in your tone
  • 📊 Analyze Spreadsheet — Upload CSV/Excel, get insights in plain English
  • 📝 Meeting Notes — Paste meeting transcript, get action items
  • 🎨 Brainstorm Ideas — Generate creative ideas for any topic

Click any one. Type or paste your content. Press Enter. Done.

✅ Welcome to private AI! Your conversation never leaves the device. No data sent to OpenAI, Google, or anyone else. You're now running a fully on-premises LLM that respects your privacy.
