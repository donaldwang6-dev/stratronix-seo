1. The Regulatory Landscape for AI in Finance

1.1 Basel III and Operational Risk Capital

Basel III's operational risk framework requires banks to identify, measure, and capitalise operational risks including third-party vendor AI dependencies. Cloud LLM providers introduce:

  • Concentration risk (single vendor for many banks)
  • Outage risk (multi-bank impact)
  • Data leakage risk (cross-bank contamination)

Each of these can attract capital add-ons of 1-10% on the operational risk base. PAA's on-premises model eliminates these add-ons.

1.2 MiFID II and Dodd-Frank Record-Keeping

Trade communications and pre-trade analytics must be retained for 5-7 years in WORM (Write Once Read Many) format. Most cloud LLM providers cannot guarantee this without significant workarounds. PAA integrates with WORM storage natively.

1.3 APRA CPS 234 (Information Security)

Australian Prudential Standard CPS 234 requires APRA-regulated entities to maintain information security capabilities commensurate with the size and complexity of their operations. CPS 234 explicitly names AI/ML systems as in-scope. PAA's local operation simplifies compliance evidence collection.
