1. Why Cloud LLMs Are Blocked in Healthcare

1.1 HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAA) Limitations

While major cloud LLM providers offer BAAs, these typically exclude:

  • Training use of customer data: Most providers reserve the right to use inputs for model improvement
  • Telemetry and logging: Diagnostic data often flows through monitoring systems outside the BAA scope
  • Sub-processor chains: Data may pass through multiple sub-processors, each with different jurisdictions
  • Cross-border inference: Models may run inference on data in different data centres per request

1.2 GDPR Article 9 (Special Category Data)

Health data is explicitly classified as "special category" under GDPR Article 9, requiring additional safeguards. Cross-border transfer of health data outside the EEA requires explicit consent or adequacy decisions, both of which add complexity for cloud APIs.

1.3 HITECH and State Laws

The HITECH Act in the US mandates breach notification for unencrypted PHI. Many US states have additional laws like California's CMIA (Confidential Medical Information Act) and Texas's HB 300 that impose further restrictions.
