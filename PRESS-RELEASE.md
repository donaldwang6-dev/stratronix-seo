### ③ Highly Stable AI Low-Level Drivers
Proprietary AI inference optimization layer delivering **7×24 industrial-grade stability with 99.99% uptime SLA**. Built for deployment scenarios where failure is not an option: hospitals, law firms, financial institutions, government agencies.

## Why It Matters Now: The 2026 Regulatory Cliff

Three regulatory shifts are making cloud LLM APIs effectively illegal for regulated industries in 2026:

1. **EU AI Act 2026** — high-risk AI systems require on-premise deployment and full data sovereignty
2. **HIPAA enforcement** — $4.2M in fines issued in 2025 for cloud-based PHI breaches
3. **South Korea's 10% Revenue-Fine Rule** — data breach fines now scale to 10% of annual revenue, effective January 2026

The STA-100 is purpose-built for organizations that need the capability of a 70B LLM but cannot — or will not — send sensitive data to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other cloud AI API.
