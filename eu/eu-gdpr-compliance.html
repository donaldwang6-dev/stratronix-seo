GDPR Compliance by Design

📍 Data Residency

All data, prompts, and AI outputs stay inside the device on your premises. Nothing leaves your office. GDPR Article 25 compliant by default.

🔒 No Third-Party API

No OpenAI / Anthropic / Google API calls. No data ever crosses borders to US hyperscalers. Schrems II safe.

🛡️ Right to Erasure

GDPR Article 17: simply delete the file. Data is on local SSD, no shadow copies in any cloud.

📊 Full Auditability

All model outputs, agent decisions, and queries are logged locally for DPIA (Article 35).

⚖️ EU AI Act Ready

High-risk AI classification, transparency obligations, and human oversight built in.

🗝️ Open Source Core

OpenClaw framework is Apache 2.0. No vendor lock-in, full code transparency.

EU AI Act Risk Classification

The EU AI Act classifies AI systems by risk level. STRATRONIX PAA is designed for limited-risk and high-risk deployments, with built-in safeguards:

  • ✅ Transparency (Article 13): All AI-generated outputs are clearly marked
  • ✅ Human Oversight (Article 14): Local UI for human-in-the-loop approval
  • ✅ Accuracy & Robustness (Article 15): Built-in Verifier agent
  • ✅ Record-Keeping (Article 12): Full local audit log
