The EU AI Act Risk Pyramid

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) classifies AI systems into four risk tiers. STRATRONIX PAA is designed to be deployed across all non-prohibited tiers with the appropriate safeguards.

🚫 Unacceptable Risk

Social scoring, manipulation, biometric categorization. STRATRONIX PAA is not designed for these use cases.

⚠️ High Risk

Healthcare, finance, legal, HR, critical infrastructure, education. STRATRONIX PAA ships with Articles 13-15 compliance tools.

ℹ️ Limited Risk

Chatbots, content generation, recommendation systems. STRATRONIX PAA provides transparency disclosures (Article 50).

✅ Minimal Risk

Internal productivity tools, personal assistants. STRATRONIX PAA free of additional obligations.

Why Cloud AI APIs Fail EU AI Act Compliance

  • ❌ Article 13: Cloud APIs don't expose model internals — deployer can't disclose
  • ❌ Article 14: Human oversight impossible with closed APIs
  • ❌ Article 11: Technical documentation owned by US hyperscaler, not deployer
  • ❌ Article 15: Cybersecurity patches at vendor's discretion
  • ❌ Data sovereignty: Training data lineage unknown for foundation models
