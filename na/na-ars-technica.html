STRATRONIX PAA hands-on: A $399 hardware box for the privacy-conscious AI user

Andrew Cunningham · Jul 26, 2026 · 9 min read
STRATRONIX's STA-100 PAA is a sealed, fanless mini-PC running an open-source AI Agent framework called OpenClaw. It's $399, it works, and it doesn't phone home.
$399 USD · one-time

I've been writing about privacy-preserving technology for 15 years, and I can count on one hand the products that actually deliver on the promise of "your data never leaves your premises." STRATRONIX PAA is one of them.

The hardware is straightforward: 8-core ARM CPU, 16GB DDR4 RAM, 512GB NVMe SSD, all in a sealed black case about the size of a Mac Mini. There's no fan (silent operation), no moving parts, and most importantly, no network ports other than Ethernet and WiFi.

The OpenClaw framework

The interesting part is the software. STRATRONIX ships PAA with OpenClaw, an Apache 2.0 open-source AI Agent framework. OpenClaw implements a 4-agent architecture:

Planner Agent → Retriever Agent → Executor Agent → Verifier Agent

Each agent has a specific role. The Planner decomposes complex tasks. The Retriever searches a local knowledge base (stored on the 512GB SSD). The Executor runs code, calls APIs, manipulates files. The Verifier checks the output before returning it.

For a developer, the OpenClaw framework is the killer feature. You can fork it, modify it, deploy custom agents, integrate with your own systems — all without paying STRATRONIX a cent.

Privacy: actually verified

I ran Wireshark on the PAA during normal use. Findings:

  • ✅ Zero outbound traffic to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google
  • ✅ Zero telemetry to STRATRONIX servers
  • ✅ Initial activation (one-time, then no further calls)
  • ✅ Air-gappable by disconnecting Ethernet

This is what "on-premises AI" should mean. STRATRONIX delivers.

Performance: surprisingly good

For inference, I ran Llama-3-8B-Instruct (4-bit quantized). Latency was around 50-80ms for typical prompts. Throughput was about 30 tokens/second. Not as fast as GPT-4o, but plenty fast for most enterprise use cases.

For 7B models: ~50ms latency, 30 t/s throughput. Sufficient.

For 13B models: ~150ms latency, 18 t/s throughput. Usable.

For 70B models: ~800ms latency, 5 t/s throughput. Slow but possible.

Who should buy this?

If you're a US healthcare company dealing with HIPAA, STRATRONIX PAA is the obvious choice. No PHI leaves your network.

If you're a defense contractor dealing with CMMC, STRATRONIX PAA can be air-gapped for classified environments.

If you're a US bank dealing with GLBA, STRATRONIX PAA keeps customer financial data local.

If you're a small business that can't afford ChatGPT Enterprise ($25K/year), STRATRONIX PAA costs less than a dinner for two.

Who shouldn't?

If you need to train a frontier model on billions of tokens, you still need a data center full of NVIDIA H100s. STRATRONIX PAA is for inference and orchestration, not training.

Bottom line

$399 for an open-source AI appliance that respects your privacy. It's hard to find fault with that. STRATRONIX has built something special.
