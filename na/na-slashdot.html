STRATRONIX Releases $399 Apache 2.0 AI Box for US Enterprises

Posted by EditorDavid · Tuesday July 28, 2026 @07:30AM · from the open-source-AI dept.
STRATRONIX Technology (Shenzhen) Company, Limited has released its $399 STA-100 Private AI-Agent Appliance (PAA) for the US market. The hardware runs the Apache 2.0 licensed OpenClaw framework, making it the first sub-$500 enterprise-grade AI appliance with full source code transparency.
ai open-source apache privacy hardware china
$399 USD · Apache 2.0

For Slashdot readers who've been skeptical of US cloud AI vendors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) — STRATRONIX PAA offers an alternative. A sealed hardware box with 8-core ARM, 16GB DDR4, and 512GB NVMe runs the open-source OpenClaw AI Agent framework entirely locally.

Wired network analysis confirms zero outbound traffic to AI vendors. The framework source is on GitHub: github.com/openclaw/openclaw (Apache 2.0, ⭐2.8K stars).
