What is STA-100 PAA?

The STA-100 Private Agent Appliance is a compact AI agent device that runs the OpenClaw intelligent agent framework. It connects to cloud LLMs (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Anthropic, etc.) while keeping all user data stored locally on the device — perfect for Government AI applications where data privacy is critical.

Why Government AI needs Private AI?

Government AI organizations handle sensitive data daily. Cloud-based AI services pose privacy, compliance, and data sovereignty risks. STA-100 PAA solves these by combining the power of cloud LLMs with local data storage.
