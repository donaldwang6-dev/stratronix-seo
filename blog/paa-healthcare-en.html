PAA for Healthcare · Vertical Edition

STRATRONIX builds on its universal PAA (Private AI-Agent Appliance) platform with a vertical edition purpose-built for healthcare. The flagship product is STA-100 Med.

What is PAA? A Private AI-Agent Appliance = local user data, cloud-side AI inference, and a 7×24 always-on local agent running on edge hardware.
STA-100 Med = universal PAA + pre-loaded medical knowledge base + HIPAA / GDPR / China Data Security Law compliance modules, ready to use out of the box.

🏥 Why Does Healthcare Need a Dedicated PAA?

Health data is the most sensitive category of personal data. A single breach can cause:

  • ⚠️ Patient privacy exposure — fines up to €20M / 4% of global revenue under GDPR
  • ⚠️ Medical errors — AI misdiagnosis can cost lives
  • ⚠️ Regulatory risk — multiple constraints from China's Data Security Law, PIPL, and Medical Device regulations
  • ⚠️ Trust collapse — patients lose confidence in the institution

Public-cloud AI is not viable (GPT / Claude / domestic LLMs — data leaves your network, violating HIPAA).
Generic AI is not viable (medical knowledge is shallow, citations to current guidelines are missing).
Custom AI is too expensive (in-house LLM build costs millions and takes 2 years).

STA-100 Med = Compliant (HIPAA / GDPR / Data Security Law) + Specialized (latest medical knowledge base) + Affordable (out-of-the-box + on-premise).

🎯 5 Healthcare Use Cases (Out of the Box)

Use Case 1 · Clinical Documentation Agent

Pain point: Doctors spend 2 hours daily writing notes, reviewing history, and reconciling test results. They spend less than 5 minutes actually seeing the patient.

PAA solution: Auto-summarize consultation audio (doctor-patient voice → structured note), auto-pull historical visits, auto-flag abnormal findings, auto-suggest ICD-10 codes. Doctor only reviews + signs off.

Use Case 2 · Medical Literature Search Agent

Pain point: For complex cases or research, clinicians must comb through 100+ recent publications (PubMed / UpToDate / Cochrane) — 3–5 hours per query.

PAA solution: Ask in natural language: "Latest 2024 treatment guidelines for HER2-positive breast cancer." PAA retrieves current literature, ranks by evidence level, extracts key findings, and produces citable paragraphs in 10 minutes.

Use Case 3 · Patient Follow-Up Agent

Pain point: Post-surgery and chronic-disease follow-up require scheduled phone / WeChat outreach. Doctors are overloaded; nurses are too.

PAA solution: Auto-trigger follow-ups per care plan (phone / WeChat / SMS). Collect structured patient feedback (pain / medication / side-effects). Escalate abnormal responses to a clinician. All patient data stays local.

Use Case 4 · Multi-Disciplinary Team (MDT) Agent

Pain point: Cancer or cardiovascular cases need oncology + radiology + pathology + surgery combined review. Material preparation takes 2–3 days; the meeting runs 1 hour.

PAA solution: Aggregate all patient data (imaging + pathology + labs + genomics). Generate an MDT briefing against the latest NCCN / ESMO / CSCO guidelines. Real-time capture of discussion points and decision rationale during the meeting.

Use Case 5 · Hospital Operations Agent

Pain point: Hospital directors review 30+ daily reports (outpatient volume / surgeries / bed turnover / insurance settlement / drug-cost ratio / consumable-cost ratio) scattered across 10+ systems.

PAA solution: Connect HIS / LIS / PACS / EMR / insurance via unified APIs. Auto-generate the executive dashboard, flag anomalies (bed shortage / insurance overrun / drug shortage), and recommend operational improvements.

🔒 Compliance Design (Healthcare-Specific)

HIPAA Compliance (US Market)

  • ✅ PHI Protection: All patient data stays on local hardware, never uploaded to public cloud
  • ✅ Access Control: Role-based granular permissions (doctor / nurse / patient self-access)
  • ✅ Audit Logs: Every data access, modification, deletion fully logged
  • ✅ Business Associate Agreement (BAA): STRATRONIX signs BAA, sharing compliance liability

GDPR Compliance (EU Market)

  • ✅ Data Minimization: Only collect what is necessary
  • ✅ Right to be Forgotten: Patients can one-click delete all their data
  • ✅ Data Portability: Patients can export their data in HL7 FHIR standard format
  • ✅ DPO Toolkit: Built-in Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) templates

China Data Security Law & PIPL Compliance

  • ✅ Data Classification: Health data = high sensitivity, automatically encrypted + audited
  • ✅ Local Storage: Domestic hospital data 100% stored locally; cross-border transfer requires CAC evaluation
  • ✅ Informed Consent: Built-in patient informed-consent templates (e-signature)
  • ✅ MLPS 2.0 Level 3: Meets Level-3 Multi-Level Protection Scheme technical requirements
