## What is PAA?

We just launched **PAA (Private AI-Agent Appliance)** — a new category of AI hardware. PAA is a **mini PC that runs AI agents entirely on-premise**. No cloud, no SaaS, no data leaks.

## Why?

Every AI agent today sends your data to someone else's cloud. For governments, hospitals, banks, law firms, and factories — this is unacceptable.

PAA fixes this by putting the AI agent execution *inside your building*. Your data never leaves.
